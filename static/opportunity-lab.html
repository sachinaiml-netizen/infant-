Design thesis

Extend existing systems only where a measurable gap is proven.

Public Prestige context already includes digital-delivery and enterprise-workflow initiatives. This prototype focuses on narrow, auditable decision workflows, not on replacing existing platforms.

Independent student prototype. Not an official Prestige Group product, endorsement or integration.
Evidence boundary: ConstructionIQ links to the working deterministic CPM engine. LaunchIQ, DocumentIQ, VendorIQ and ServiceIQ are interactive concept demos using invented records and transparent rules. No Prestige project, customer, supplier, financial or service data is included. No regulatory prediction or automatic operational decision is made.
